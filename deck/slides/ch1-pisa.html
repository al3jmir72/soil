CHAPTER 1 · A FAMOUS FOUNDATION PROBLEM

Why the Tower of Pisa leans

  • Begun in 1173, built in stages over 200+ years.
  • About 15,700 t on a base roughly 20 m wide.
  • A weak clay layer about 11 m deep compressed unevenly.
  • Over 5 m out of plumb on 54 m: about 5.5°.
  • Fixed by removing about 70 t of soil under the north side: tilt now about 5°.

Bologna: Garisenda 48 m, tilt about 4°. Asinelli 97 m, about 1.3°.

Chapter 1 · History

The lesson: soft clay under a heavy tower settles unevenly, so the tower tilts. Remember the numbers: 1173, about 11 metres of weak clay, tilt about 5.5 degrees, about 70 tonnes of soil removed from the north side. The animation runs in Present mode.
