CHAPTER 3 · PROBLEM RECIPE

Five steps for any problem

1

Sketch

Draw air, water and solids. Volumes left, weights right.

2

Pick a basis

Vs = 1 if you know e. V = 1 if you know n. Or use the given volume.

3

Fill in

Use Gs, w and γw to write every box you can.

4

Link

Use S·e = w·Gs to tie saturation to the rest.

5

Check units

γw = 9.81 kN/m³ (62.4 lb/ft³). ρw = 1000 kg/m³.

Typical Gs: quartz sand about 2.65, clays and silts 2.6 to 2.9.

Density to unit weight: γ (kN/m³) = 9.81 × ρ (kg/m³) ÷ 1000.

Chapter 3 · Weight–volume

Do not skip step one. Most lost marks come from trying to do it in your head. Step five saves you from mixing kilograms with kilonewtons.
