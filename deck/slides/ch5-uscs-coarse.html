CHAPTER 5 · USCS COARSE-GRAINED

Sands and gravels: count the fines

First letter: G gravel · S sand · M silt · C clay · O organic · Pt peat. Second letter: W well graded · P poorly graded · L low plasticity (LL < 50) · H high plasticity (LL ≥ 50)

Under 5% fines

CLEAN: look at the curve

GW or SW needs Cu ≥ 4 (gravel) or ≥ 6 (sand) and 1 ≤ Cc ≤ 3.

Otherwise GP or SP.

5% to 12% fines

BORDERLINE: dual symbol

Gradation first, then the fines type.

Examples: SP-SC, GW-GM.

Over 12% fines

DIRTY: look at the fines

GM or SM: PI < 4, or plots below the A-line.

GC or SC: PI > 7 and on or above the A-line.

Coarse means more than 50% is retained on No. 200. Gravel if more than half of the coarse part is retained on No. 4, otherwise sand.

Group name: add “with sand” (for gravels) or “with gravel” (for sands) when the other coarse part is 15% or more.

Chapter 5 · Classification

Gravel: passes 76.2 mm, retained on No. 4 (4.75 mm). Sand: passes No. 4, retained on No. 200 (0.075 mm). If fines are 4 to 7 in PI and fall in the hatched zone of the plasticity chart, the dirty case becomes GC-GM or SC-SM. Examples: 5.9 gives SP (Cu 3.44, Cc 1.59, 3 percent fines) and 5.10 gives GW (Cu 36, Cc 2.25).
