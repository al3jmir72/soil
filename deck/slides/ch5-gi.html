CHAPTER 5 · GROUP INDEX

The group index: a subgrade score

GI = (F200 − 35)[0.2 + 0.005 (LL − 40)] + 0.01 (F200 − 15)(PI − 10)

Higher GI means a worse subgrade. Write it in brackets: A-6(8).

Negative result → GI = 0.

Round to the nearest whole number (3.4 → 3, 3.5 → 4).

GI = 0 for A-1-a, A-1-b, A-2-4, A-2-5, A-3.

A-2-6 and A-2-7: use only 0.01 (F200 − 15)(PI − 10).

Example 5.3

F200 = 95, LL = 60, PI = 40 → A-7-6. GI = 18 + 24 = 42, so A-7-6(42).

Example 5.5

F200 = 58, LL = 30, PI = 10 → A-4. GI = 23 × 0.15 = 3.45 → 3. The book prints 4: ask which your course uses.

Chapter 5 · Classification

The first term of the formula is the partial group index from LL, the second is from PI. F200 is percent passing sieve number 200; LL and PI are of the fraction passing number 40. Example 5.3 step by step: 95 minus 35 is 60; 0.2 plus 0.005 times 20 is 0.3; 60 times 0.3 is 18. Then 0.01 times 80 times 30 is 24.
