Chapter 2 · Origin of soil

Memorise the USCS row and the sieve numbers. USCS gravel is 76.2 to 4.75 millimetres, sand is 4.75 to 0.075. AASHTO and USDA put the gravel-sand boundary at 2 millimetres. The 0.075 millimetre line is sieve number 200.
