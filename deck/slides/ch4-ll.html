Casagrande cup (ASTM D-4318)

Cup drops 10 mm. LL = water content when the groove closes 12.5 mm after 25 blows. Run 3 or more tests with N from 15 to 35, then plot w against log N (the flow curve).

Fall cone (BS 1377)

30° cone, 80 g (0.78 N). LL = water content at 20 mm penetration in 5 s. Run 4 or more tests and plot w against the penetration d.

One point: LL = wN (N / 25)^0.121

Flow index: IF = (w1 − w2) / log(N2 / N1)

Chapter 4 · Plasticity

The cup method is the standard one. Example 4.1: points (15 blows, 42.0 percent), (20, 40.8) and (28, 39.3) give LL about 39.5 percent and flow index about 9.96. Example 4.2: one test, N equals 20, w equals 40.8 percent gives LL equals 40.8 times 20 over 25 to the power 0.121, which is 39.7 percent. At the liquid limit the undrained shear strength is roughly 2 kilopascals.
