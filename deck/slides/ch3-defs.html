CHAPTER 3 · DEFINITIONS

Six ratios that run the chapter

e = Vv / Vs

Void ratio. Can be above 1 (soft organic clays).

n = Vv / V

Porosity. Always below 1 (below 100%).

S = Vw / Vv

Degree of saturation. 0% is dry, 100% is saturated.

w = Ww / Ws

Water content. Divide by the weight of SOLIDS.

γ = W / V

Moist (total) unit weight = Ws (1 + w) / V.

γd = Ws / V

Dry unit weight, and γd = γ / (1 + w).

e = n / (1 − n) n = e / (1 + e) γw = 9.81 kN/m³

Chapter 3 · Weight–volume

Teal cards are volume ratios, terracotta cards are weight ratios. Two classic traps: water content divides by the weight of solids, not total weight; and porosity is always less than one while void ratio may exceed one. Densities in kg per cubic metre convert to kilonewtons per cubic metre by multiplying by 9.81 and dividing by 1000.
