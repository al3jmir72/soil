Chapter 2 · Origin of soil

Three rock types: igneous from cooling magma, sedimentary from deposited and cemented sediments, metamorphic from heat and pressure. Click to walk around the loop. Intrusive igneous rocks cool slowly underground and have big crystals. Extrusive ones cool fast at the surface and are fine-grained.
