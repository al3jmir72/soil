Chapter 1 · History

Periods come from Skempton (1985). Preclassical 1700 to 1776. Classical phase one 1776 to 1856, when Coulomb and the French school built rigorous earth-pressure theory. Classical phase two 1856 to 1910, mostly lab work on sand. Modern 1910 to 1927, when clay behaviour was worked out. The hook helps you order them: guesswork, then Coulomb's maths, then sand in the lab, then clay.
