The five chapters flow in a line. Chapter 1 is history, which is mostly names and dates. Chapter 2 explains where soil comes from and how we measure grain size. Chapter 3 gives the maths of the three-phase soil. Chapter 4 covers plasticity. Chapter 5 puts soils into classification boxes using what you learned in chapters 2 and 4.
