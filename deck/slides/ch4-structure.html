CHAPTER 4 · SOIL STRUCTURE

How the grains are arranged

Sands: packing equal spheres (void ratio e)

Simple cubical · e = 0.91

Simple stagger · 0.65

Double stagger · 0.43

Pyramidal · 0.35

Honeycombed

Fine sand and silt in small arches. Big voids; collapses under heavy or shock loads.

Clays: forces between particles

Dispersed

Particles roughly parallel. Repulsion wins.

Flocculent

Edge-to-face contact, or salt cutting repulsion. Open, high void ratio. Marine clays are highly flocculent.

Domains → clusters → peds

Chapter 4 · Plasticity

Idealised sand packings: simple cubical 0.91, simple stagger 0.65, double stagger 0.43, pyramidal 0.35. Real soils have a similar range because small grains fill voids while irregular shapes raise e. For clays, double layers repel, van der Waals forces attract, and positively charged edges can stick to negative faces.
