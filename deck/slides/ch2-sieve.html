CHAPTER 2 · SIEVE ANALYSIS

Shake, weigh, repeat

  1. Oven-dry the soil. Stack sieves: coarsest on top, pan at the bottom.
  2. Shake, then weigh what stays on each sieve.
  3. Add up the mass retained so far, then subtract from the total.

% finer = (total − cumulative retained) ÷ total × 100

Plot % finer on a normal axis against grain size on a log axis. Sieves work above 0.075 mm (No. 200).

Chapter 2 · Origin of soil

Big grains get stuck on the top sieve, finer ones fall further. The pan collects everything finer than the smallest sieve. For cohesive soils, wash the soil through the sieves as a slurry first. The smallest sieve in this test is number 200, 0.075 millimetres. Below that you switch to the hydrometer.
