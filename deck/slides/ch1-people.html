CHAPTER 1 · WHO DID WHAT

Eight names to know cold

Coulomb

1776

Sliding wedge with friction and cohesion

Rankine

1857

Simplified Coulomb’s earth-pressure theory

Darcy

1856

Permeability law from sand-filter tests

Boussinesq

1885

Stress under a load in an elastic half-space

Atterberg

about 1908–1911

Clay fraction (< 2 µm); liquid, plastic, shrinkage limits

Fellenius

1918 and 1926

Circular slip surfaces; φ = 0 for saturated clay slopes

Terzaghi

1925

Consolidation theory; book Erdbaumechanik

Skempton

1948 and 1954

φ = 0 concept; pore-pressure parameters A and B

Also: Poncelet (1840, the symbol φ), Collin (1846, clay slips), Reynolds (1887, dilatancy)

Chapter 1 · History

If you only have ten minutes, memorise this slide. Terzaghi is the father of modern soil mechanics: consolidation theory in 1925, and he presided over the first ISSMFE conference at Harvard in 1936. Skempton's phi equals zero concept came at the 1948 Rotterdam conference.
