CHAPTER 4 · PLASTIC LIMIT AND PI

Where plasticity starts and how big it is

PLASTIC LIMIT (PL)

3.2 mm

Roll the soil into a thread by hand on a glass plate. PL is the water content when the thread crumbles at 3.2 mm (1/8 in.).

PI = LL − PL

The width of the plastic range. It drives the plasticity chart.

0 · Nonplastic

1–5 · Slightly plastic

5–10 · Low plasticity

10–20 · Medium plasticity

20–40 · High plasticity

Above 40 · Very high plasticity

Burmister scale, PI in %

Chapter 4 · Plasticity

PL is the lower edge of the plastic range, LL is the upper edge. Fall-cone alternative for PL uses a heavier cone: 240 g, 2.35 N, again 20 mm penetration. Useful correlations: PI equals 4.12 times the flow index from the cup test.
