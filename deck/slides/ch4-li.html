CI = (LL − w) / (LL − PL)

Chapter 4 · Plasticity

Natural water content w compared to PL and LL. If w is above the liquid limit, LI is above one: the clay is sensitive and can flow like a viscous liquid once remolded. If w is below PL, LI is negative, typical of heavily overconsolidated deposits.
