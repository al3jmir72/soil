CHAPTER 3 · THE GOLDEN RELATION

One equation links everything

S · e = w · Gs

Where it comes from: take Vs = 1

STEP 1

Vs = 1, so Vv = e and V = 1 + e

STEP 2

Ws = Gs · γw

STEP 3

Vw = S · e, so Ww = S · e · γw

STEP 4

w = Ww / Ws = S·e / Gs, so S·e = w·Gs

DRY

γd = Gs γw / (1 + e)

MOIST

γ = (1 + w) Gs γw / (1 + e)

SATURATED

γsat = (Gs + e) γw / (1 + e)

Saturated (S = 1): e = w·Gs. Dry (S = 0): w = 0.

Chapter 3 · Weight–volume

Taking Vs equal to one is a trick, not physics: it makes every other quantity easy to write. If instead you know the porosity n, take total volume V equal to one. Then gamma dry is Gs gamma w times one minus n. The golden relation works for any soil. Learn the three unit-weight formulas below it: they all share the denominator one plus e.
