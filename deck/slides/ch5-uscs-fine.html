Chapter 5 · Classification

Fine-grained means 50 percent or more passes sieve number 200. Use the plasticity chart from Chapter 4 with LL and PI of the fraction passing No. 40. Organic soils (OL, OH) are identified by the ratio of oven-dried LL to not-dried LL being below 0.75. Peat is Pt. Example 5.4: 58 percent fines, LL 30, PI 10 gives CL, sandy lean clay.
