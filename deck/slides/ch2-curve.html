Grain size (mm, log scale)

Poorly (uniformly) graded: steep curve, grains almost all one size.

Well graded: gentle curve, wide range of sizes. Needs Cu > 4 (gravel) or > 6 (sand), and 1 ≤ Cc ≤ 3.

Gap graded (dashed): a flat part means a size range is missing.

Cu = D60 / D10

Cc = D30² / (D60 × D10)

Chapter 2 · Origin of soil

Dx means the grain size at which x percent of the soil is finer. D10 is called the effective size and tells you about drainage. Cu measures the spread, Cc the shape. Both are needed: well graded needs Cu above 4 for gravels or above 6 for sands, and Cc between 1 and 3.
