Plot PI on the vertical axis against LL on the horizontal axis. This chart is the foundation of the USCS fine-grained classification in Chapter 5. Estimating the shrinkage limit from the chart: extend the A-line and U-line down until they meet at LL minus 43.5, PI minus 46.4; draw a line from that point through your soil's point and read where it hits PI equals zero.
