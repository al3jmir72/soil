CHAPTER 4 · SHRINKAGE LIMIT

When drying stops shrinking the soil

Volume

SL

Water content

no more shrinking

volume shrinks as w drops

SL = wi − Δw

start water content minus water lost while shrinking

wi = (M1 − M2) / M2 × 100

M1 = wet mass of the pat, M2 = dry mass

Δw = (Vi − Vf) ρw / M2 × 100

Vi = initial volume, Vf = dry volume

SR = M2 / (Vf ρw) Gs = 1 / (1/SR − SL/100)

shrinkage ratio, then specific gravity of solids

Example 4.5: SL = 46.18 − 28.9 = 17.28%. Example 4.6: SR = 30.1 / 15.9 = 1.89 and Gs ≈ 2.81. Typical SL: montmorillonite 8.5–15, illite 15–17, kaolinite 25–29.

Chapter 4 · Plasticity

Above SL the soil shrinks as it dries. Below SL the volume no longer changes. Test: fill a dish with wet soil, record wet mass and volume, oven-dry, then find the dry volume by wax coating and weighing in water. Volumetric shrinkage at water content w is SR times w minus SL.
