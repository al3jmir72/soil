Chapter 5 · Classification

The 50 percent versus 35 percent split is a favourite. A coarse soil with about 35 percent fines already behaves like a fine-grained one, which is why many engineers find the AASHTO threshold more realistic. The equivalence is approximate, from Liu (1967).
