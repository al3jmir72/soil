Chapter 2 · Origin of soil

More specific surface means more negative charge and more water held, so more swelling. Montmorillonite is the troublemaker, kaolinite is the well-behaved one. Clay minerals are aluminium silicates built from silica tetrahedra and alumina octahedra sheets. Clay particles carry a net negative charge and attract a diffuse double layer of water, which gives plasticity.
