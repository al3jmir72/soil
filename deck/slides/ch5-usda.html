Soil A

1. Gravel? Re-scale first

Modified % = (% of that fraction ÷ (100 − % gravel)) × 100

2. Plot the three percentages

Sand 2–0.05 mm, silt 0.05–0.002 mm, clay below 0.002 mm. They sum to 100.

3. Read the zone

Clay loam, sandy loam, silty clay … Add “gravelly” when gravel is significant.

Example 5.1, soil A

Gravel 12, sand 25, silt 32, clay 31 → 28.4 / 36.4 / 35.2 → gravelly clay loam
