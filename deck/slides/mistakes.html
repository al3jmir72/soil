EXAM TRAPS

Six mistakes that cost marks

WATER CONTENT

Dividing by total weight.

Divide by the weight of solids: w = Ww / Ws.

GRADATION

Checking only Cu.

Check Cu and Cc (1 to 3). Gravel Cu ≥ 4, sand Cu ≥ 6.

UNITS

Mixing kg/m³ with kN/m³.

γ = 9.81 × ρ / 1000.

USDA GRAVEL

Plotting raw percentages when gravel is present.

Re-scale by 100 − % gravel first.

THRESHOLDS

Mixing up AASHTO and USCS.

AASHTO 35% and No. 10. USCS 50% and No. 4.

ATTERBERG TESTS

Using the whole sample.

LL and PI are for the fraction passing No. 40.

Exam strategy

For every calculation question: write the formula, substitute with units, box the answer. Partial marks live in the first two.
