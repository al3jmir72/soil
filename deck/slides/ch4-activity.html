Chapter 4 · Plasticity

Skempton found that PI grows linearly with the clay-size fraction, and the slope of that line is the activity. Steeper slope means a more active clay that swells more. The three lines on the chart use typical values; real montmorillonite ranges from 1.5 to 7.
