CHAPTER 1 · DEFINITIONS

Four words you must define

Soil

Uncemented grains of mineral and decayed organic matter, with liquid and gas in the gaps between them.

Soil mechanics

The science of soil’s physical properties and how a mass of soil behaves under forces.

Soils engineering

Putting soil-mechanics principles to work on practical problems.

Geotechnical engineering

Soil mechanics plus rock mechanics, applied to foundations, retaining structures and earth structures.

Soil is both a construction material and a foundation material.

Chapter 1 · History

Exam tip: geotechnical engineering is the umbrella term. It covers soil AND rock mechanics. Soil is both what you build with (embankments) and what you build on (foundations).
