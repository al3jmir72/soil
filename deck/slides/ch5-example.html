CHAPTER 5 · WORKED EXAMPLE

One soil, two names

58% passes No. 200, LL = 30, PI = 10, no gravel.

AASHTO

1. 58% > 35% passes No. 200, so silt–clay.

2. LL = 30 ≤ 40 and PI = 10 ≤ 10, so A-4.

3. GI = (58 − 35)[0.2 + 0.005 (30 − 40)] + 0.01 (58 − 15)(10 − 10) = 23 × 0.15 = 3.45.

4. Round to the nearest whole number: 3 (the book prints 4).

USCS

1. 58% ≥ 50% passes No. 200, so fine-grained.

2. LL = 30 < 50. A-line at LL 30 is 0.73 × 10 = 7.3. PI = 10 is above it, so CL.

3. Retained on No. 200 is 42%, which is 30% or more, and sand (42%) beats gravel (0%).

4. Prefix “sandy”.

A-4(3)

CL: sandy lean clay

Chapter 5 · Classification

This is the soil from examples 5.4 and 5.5 in the book. If your instructor rounds 3.45 up, write A-4(4); by the rounding rule in the chapter (nearest whole number) it is A-4(3). Be ready to state which convention you used.
