Chapter 3 · Weight–volume

Soil element: air on top, water in the middle, solids at the bottom. Volumes: V equals Vs plus Vw plus Va. Voids Vv equals Vw plus Va. Weights: W equals Ws plus Ww, because air weighs almost nothing. Dry soil has no water, saturated soil has no air, moist soil has both.
