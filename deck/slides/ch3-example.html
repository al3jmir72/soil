STEP 1 · MASS OF SOLIDS

Ms = M / (1 + w) = 2350 / 1.086 = 2163.9 kg

STEP 2 · VOLUME OF SOLIDS

Vs = Ms / (Gs ρw) = 2163.9 / 2710 = 0.798 m³

STEP 3 · VOIDS

Vv = 1.2 − 0.798 = 0.402 m³, e = 0.503, n = 0.335

STEP 4 · WATER AND SATURATION

Mw = 186.1 kg, Vw = 0.186 m³, S = 0.186 / 0.402 = 46.3%

Given: V = 1.2 m³, M = 2350 kg, w = 8.6%, Gs = 2.71. Find e, n, S.

Chapter 3 · Weight–volume

This is Example 3.2 from the book. Check with the golden relation: S equals w times Gs over e, which is 0.086 times 2.71 over 0.503, about 46.3 percent. Also moist density is 1958.3 kg per cubic metre and dry density is 1803.3.
