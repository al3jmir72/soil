# 16-Day Study Plan to 9 Nov

Sixteen study days from 24 Oct lead to the exam on 9 Nov 2026: one chapter block at a time, then a timed mock and a light final day.

- [ ] **Day 2, Sun 25 Oct: Chapter 2, part 1.** Rock cycle, weathering, residual vs transported soil, then the clay-mineral comparison (specific surface 15, 80, 800 m²/g). About 2 h.
- [ ] **Day 3, Mon 26 Oct: Chapter 2, part 2.** Size limits for USDA, AASHTO and USCS, the sieve test, percent finer, Cu and Cc. Redo Example 2.1 on paper (answers: Cu 4.56, Cc 0.93). About 2 h.

## Daily routine and falling behind

Use the same four steps every day and the slides, cheat sheet and notes will line up with the plan.

1. **Learn (40 min):** play the day's slides in Present mode so the animations and the live demos run; pause on each worked example.
2. **Say it (20 min):** explain the day's idea out loud as if teaching a friend; where you stall, reread the matching cheat-sheet section.
3. **Do it (40 min):** redo the day's worked example on paper, then check against the answer.
4. **Test (20 min):** do the chapter's quick-check slide, hide the answers, and write down any miss.

If you fall behind, drop the Chapter 1 history detail first and keep its four periods and key names. Never skip the Day 14 mock; move it rather than delete it. Re-take any quick check you missed two days later, because spaced repetition beats re-reading.
